This is the third edition of the London Operations Research Day (LORD), which brings together researchers in operations and associated fields from the London area. LORD is a single-track workshop consisting of a limited number of invited presentations and ample free time for interaction and discussion among participants.
The workshop will be held at Imperial Business School, on Friday, April 17, 2026.

The workshop will also feature a poster session to give an opportunity to PhD students/postdocs/junior faculty to present their work. Those wishing to apply to apply for the poster session will need to fill out a form (to be announced). Acceptance notifications will subsequently be sent out along with instructions.
